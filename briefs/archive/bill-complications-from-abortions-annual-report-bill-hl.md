**What are we asking for in the petition?**

We are calling on supporters to urge members of the House of Lords to back the Complications from Abortions (Annual Report) Bill [HL], currently awaiting its 2nd reading, so that Parliament requires an annual public report on the complications women suffer from abortions.

**Why would they listen to us?**

Peers are actively engaged on this issue, as shown by the volume of debate, motions and written questions in recent months. Lord Moylan has brought this Bill forward and needs visible public backing to demonstrate that this is not a niche concern but one that matters deeply to ordinary people across the country.

**What is happening that we are responding to?**

Complications from Abortions (Annual Report) Bill [HL] (sponsor: Lord Moylan) is at 2nd reading in the Lords. Next key date: TBA. Parliamentary activity on this issue in the last six months (from the monitor's ledger): 70 debate contributions, 2 motions, 9 motion signatures, 7 written questions.

**What is the key point of injustice that is at stake here?**

Right now there is no legal requirement for the government to publish a full, honest annual account of the physical and psychological complications women experience after abortions. Women are being denied the transparency they deserve, while the true human cost of abortion is left undocumented and unspoken in the corridors of power.

## Prepare phase prompts

**What are some arguments supporting our point of view?**

- Basic patient safety demands that complications from any medical procedure, including abortion, are properly recorded and reported.
- Without annual, transparent reporting, women cannot make truly informed choices, and policymakers cannot make truly informed decisions.
- This Bill does not restrict access to abortion. It simply asks for honesty and accountability, something no one should be afraid of unless they have something to hide.
- Every other area of medicine is subject to outcome reporting and audit. Abortion should be no exception.

**Why is it urgent that we take action now?**

The Bill is at 2nd reading in the House of Lords right now. This is a critical early stage where peers are forming their views, and parliamentary attention is active, with 70 debate contributions, 2 motions and 7 written questions on this issue in the last six months alone. Supporters need to make their voices heard before momentum is lost and the Bill quietly slips down the agenda.

**Describe a bad outcome if we do not win this campaign:**

If this Bill fails to progress, women will continue to be denied honest information about the real risks and complications of abortion, hidden from public record and parliamentary scrutiny. The silence around the harm abortion can cause to women will continue, and future safety failures may go unnoticed and unaddressed.

**Describe a good outcome if we do win this campaign:**

If this Bill succeeds, the government will be required to publish an annual report on complications from abortions, bringing long overdue transparency and accountability. Women will finally have access to honest data, and Parliament will have the evidence it needs to properly protect women's health and safety.

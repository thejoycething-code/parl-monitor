**What are we asking for in the petition?**

We are asking MPs to vote against the Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill at Second Reading in the House of Commons, and to reject any move to legalise assisted suicide for terminally ill people in England and Wales. Supporters are being asked to write directly to their MP before the bill's next Commons stage, due 11 September 2026, urging them to vote No.

**What is happening that we are responding to?**

Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill (sponsor: Lauren Edwards) is at 2nd reading in the Commons. Next key date: 2026-09-11. Parliamentary activity on this issue in the last six months (from the monitor's ledger): 345 debate contributions, 1 written questions.

**What is the key point of injustice that is at stake here?**

This Bill would open the door to state-sanctioned killing of the sick and dying, dressed up as compassion. Once assisted dying is written into law, it does not stay contained. The most vulnerable, the elderly, the disabled, the depressed, those who feel they are a burden, become exposed to a quiet pressure to end their lives rather than be cared for. That is not mercy. That is abandonment.

## Prepare phase prompts

**What are some arguments supporting our point of view?**

- Every doctor's duty is to heal and to comfort, never to kill. This Bill asks the medical profession to cross a line that should never be crossed.
- Countries and states that have legalised assisted dying have seen eligibility criteria widen over time, drawing in people who were never the original target group. [VERIFY: specific comparative evidence]
- Genuine compassion means investing in palliative care and human presence at the end of life, not offering death as a service.
- Vulnerable people, the disabled, the elderly, those without family support, are placed at real risk once assisted suicide becomes a legal option rather than an unthinkable act.
- Life has inherent dignity and worth from beginning to natural end. No Parliament has the right to legislate that some lives are no longer worth living.

**Why is it urgent that we take action now?**

This Bill is already at Second Reading in the Commons, with 345 debate contributions logged in the last six months alone. Momentum is building in Parliament, and the next key date is 11 September 2026. MPs need to hear from constituents now, before this Bill advances any further. Silence will be read as consent.

**Why would they listen to us?**

MPs at Second Reading are still forming their final view and are highly sensitive to constituency pressure on a conscience issue like this. A strong, personal wave of letters from real constituents, especially those who know the fear and reality of terminal illness, carries real weight on a free vote of this kind.

**Describe a bad outcome if we do not win this campaign:**

If this Bill passes, England and Wales will have crossed an irreversible line. Assisted suicide becomes normalised, vulnerable and elderly people feel pressure to request death rather than seek care, and the medical profession is reshaped around the taking of life rather than the protection of it. Once the law is changed, it will not be changed back.

**Describe a good outcome if we do win this campaign:**

MPs vote down the Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill. The principle that every human life is worth protecting to its natural end is upheld in British law. Resources and political attention turn instead to strengthening palliative and hospice care, so that no one facing terminal illness ever feels that death is their only option.

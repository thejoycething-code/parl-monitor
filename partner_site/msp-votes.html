How your MSPs voted

In March 2026 the Scottish Parliament rejected the Assisted Dying for Terminally Ill Adults (Scotland) Bill, by 69 votes to 57. This page shows how each sitting MSP voted at every stage the Parliament divided on. The Bill was decided in the LAST Parliament: Scotland went to the polls in May 2026, so 64 of the 129 sitting MSPs have a vote on it and the other 65 were not yet members. These are facts, not judgements — and where an MSP changed their mind between the first vote and the last, it says so.

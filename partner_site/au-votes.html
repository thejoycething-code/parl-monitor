How did your MP or senator vote?

Recorded divisions in the House of Representatives and the Senate on the issues we track, from Hansard, with each member's speeches, motions and notices on the same ground. Search by name, electorate or state, or filter by chamber, party and electorate or state.

Built from Hansard as OpenAustralia publishes it (divisions, speeches), the Parliamentary Handbook (PHIDs) and the APH's bill pages. Whether a vote was for or against our side is a human judgement, made per division and shown only once it is signed off; a division without a signed reading shows the record with no judgement. Party is the party at the vote. A pair is shown as a pair: Hansard lists who was paired but not which side each partner was on.

How did your TD or senator vote?

Recorded divisions in the Dáil, the Seanad and their committees on the issues we track, from the Oireachtas's own records, with each member's Private Members' bills, parliamentary questions and speeches on the same ground. Search by name or constituency, or filter by House, party and constituency.

Built from the Houses of the Oireachtas Open Data (divisions, members, bills and sponsors, parliamentary questions and debates). Whether a vote was for or against our side is a human judgement, made per division and shown only once it is signed off; a division without a signed reading shows the record with no judgement. Party is the party at the vote. Committee divisions are taken among a committee's members only: a member with no position on one was not on that committee.

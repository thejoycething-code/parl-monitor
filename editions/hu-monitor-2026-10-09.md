> Bills and recorded votes await the Országgyűlés's API token (HU1): parlament.hu answers this project with a CAPTCHA, which is never solved or worked around. Until then this edition reports what became law, from the official gazette, a week or more after the vote and without positions.

> **How to read this edition.** Items come from the Hungary store, classified by taxonomy-hu v0.1 (approved without a native read, X4) and config/watchlist-hu.yaml. The AI judge is off (X16): nothing has been read for relevance by a model, items are ordered by tier (watched and tier 1 first), and a tier-2 match can still be noise. Titles are the source's own Hungarian, verbatim; the English around them is ours. Tallies, results and splits are the record; whether a vote helped or hurt is a human call and is never made here. No member positions: this edition reads the official gazette, which records what became law, not who voted. Migration is matched and stored but not shown. Procedural votes, the patterns in config/edition-noise-hu.yaml and Chris's mutes are left out (counted under Coverage); a watched item never is.

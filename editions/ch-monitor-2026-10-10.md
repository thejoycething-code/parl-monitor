> **How to read this edition.** Items come from the Switzerland store, classified by taxonomy-atch v0.1, taxonomy-fr v0.2 (approved without a native read, X4) and config/watchlist-ch.yaml. The AI judge is off (X16): nothing has been read for relevance by a model, items are ordered by tier (watched and tier 1 first), and a tier-2 match can still be noise. Titles are the source's own German or French, verbatim; the English around them is ours. Tallies, results and splits are the record; whether a vote helped or hurt is a human call and is never made here. Both councils record every member's position, with the Fraktion at the vote; the Nationalrat's tallies are counted from those positions. Migration is matched and stored but not shown. Procedural votes, the patterns in config/edition-noise-ch.yaml and Chris's mutes are left out (counted under Coverage); a watched item never is.

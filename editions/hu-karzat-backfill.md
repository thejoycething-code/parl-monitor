## How to read it

- Classified by taxonomy-hu v0.2 on each paper's Hungarian title; a vote takes its paper's areas. No model read anything (X16); a tier-2 match can be noise.
- Groups are the parliament's own, at the vote. Positions are stored as recorded (igen, nem, tartózkodott, and the ways of not voting); “against their group's majority” counts igen and nem only, so an abstention is never named. Which way a vote cut is a human call, never made here.
- Migration is stored but not shown (area 11).

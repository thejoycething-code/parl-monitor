> **How to read this edition.** Items come from the Netherlands store, classified by taxonomy-nl v0.1 (approved without a native read, X4) and config/watchlist-nl.yaml. The AI judge is off (X16): nothing has been read for relevance by a model, items are ordered by tier (watched and tier 1 first), and a tier-2 match can still be noise. Titles are the source's own Dutch, verbatim; the English around them is ours. Tallies, results and splits are the record; whether a vote helped or hurt is a human call and is never made here. Most votes are by show of hands, one position per fractie; member positions shown for them are DERIVED from the fractie vote (X5), given to every member the store lists under that fractie by their latest fractie. Roll calls record every member. Migration is matched and stored but not shown. Procedural votes, the patterns in config/edition-noise-nl.yaml and Chris's mutes are left out (counted under Coverage); a watched item never is.

## On our ground (1)

### Revision of Schengen short-stay visa procedures (recast)

- **Areas:** Migration | **Closes:** 2026-10-06 (10 days, AMBER) | **Type:** PROP_REG
- As set out in the EU Visa Strategy, the Commission will bring forward a legislative recast of the EU Visa Code. The initiative will make Schengen visa rules more strategic, coherent and effective, strengthening tools on security, migration, readmission and third-country cooperation, while facilitating legitimate travel and clarifying technical aspects linked to digital visa procedures.
- **Why it matters:** Tighter Schengen visa rules affect border control coherence across Europe and could shape UK-adjacent migration policy debates on integrity and enforcement. (score 2)
- Respond: https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/19222

## Notes

- On our ground means what the Brazil edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: The party the vote record names at each vote. Beside each Câmara position, the orientation the party (or its federation) gave for that vote, as the Câmara publishes it (orientação de bancada): the only whip line any of these parliaments publishes.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-br.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.
- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; br-historico read 17 October 2026: the Câmara's status history of each sitting deputy and the Senado's mandates of each sitting senator (each chamber separately: a deputy's Senate years, or a senator's Câmara years, are not linked)). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- Generated by tools/member_profiles.py from the store each week; do not edit by hand.

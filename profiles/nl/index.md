## Notes

- On our ground means what the Netherlands edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: Most Tweede Kamer votes are by show of hands, one position per fractie; those member positions are DERIVED (X5), given to every member the store lists under that fractie by their latest fractie. Roll calls record each member and are shown as recorded. Since 11 October 2026, where the parliament dates a member's seats and group (src/member_service.py), a DERIVED position is given only for a day the member sat, by the group they were in that day; otherwise by the latest group, as labelled.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-nl.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.
- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; nl-fractiezetels read 11 October 2026: every Tweede Kamer fractie seat the gegevensmagazijn records (the earliest from 1 November 1977)). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- DERIVED positions follow the record of who sat: where the parliament dates a member's seats and group, a member is given a group's vote only for a day they sat, and by the group they were in that day ('group's vote on the day'); 2280 position(s) the store's derivation by today's group would have given were left out on that ground. A member without dated rows is derived by their latest group, as labelled.
- Committees, groups and declared interests: the parliament's own open data (src/member_intel.py; nl-commissies read 2026-10-11 (1415 row(s)); nl-nevenfuncties read 2026-10-11 (387 row(s))). A membership is shown as recorded; a group is flagged on our ground by its name only (config/member-intel-groups.yaml).
- Attendance: sittings from the parliament's own open data (src/attendance.py; nl-activiteiten read 2026-10-11 (since 2025-11-12)). Statuses are the record's own; excused absences are counted apart; nothing is inferred where a record lists no absences.
- Generated by tools/member_profiles.py from the store each week; do not edit by hand.

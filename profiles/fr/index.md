## Notes

- On our ground means what the France edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: The political group the scrutin names at each vote.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-fr.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.
- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; fr-amo30 read 17 October 2026: every Assemblée nationale mandate from the 12th legislature (from 19 June 2002; AMO30)). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- Committees, groups and declared interests: the parliament's own open data (src/member_intel.py; fr-hatvp read 2026-10-17 (7337 row(s)); fr-organes read 2026-10-17 (6610 row(s))). A membership is shown as recorded; a group is flagged on our ground by its name only (config/member-intel-groups.yaml). Declared interests in France: the HATVP's declarations of interests and activities (open data, Licence Ouverte), the member's own items only; spouses' and collaborators' activities are never read.
- Attendance: sittings from the parliament's own open data (src/attendance.py; fr-agenda read 2026-10-17 (Agenda.json.zip, legislature 17)). Statuses are the record's own; excused absences are counted apart; nothing is inferred where a record lists no absences. 268 member(s) in the attendance record matched no listed member and are not shown.

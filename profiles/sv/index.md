## Notes

- On our ground means what the El Salvador edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: The party the vote PDF names at each vote. The PDFs print names only, matched to the member list by name (a short form such as 'ADOLFO RIVAS' only when exactly one listed member's name contains it); a name that matches no one, or more than one, is shown as printed, as are former deputies.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-sv.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.

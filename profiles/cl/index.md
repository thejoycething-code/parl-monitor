## Notes

- On our ground means what the Chile edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: Deputies: the party on the day, from the Cámara's militancias. Senators: the party on the day from the BCN's party history where it covers the vote (X6), otherwise the party when the vote was collected, as listed.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-cl.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.
- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; cl-militancias read 11 October 2026: the Cámara's militancias (dated party spells it records for each sitting deputy's time in the chamber), joined end to end; senators not read). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- Attendance: sittings from the parliament's own open data (src/attendance.py; cl-comisiones read 2026-10-11 (2 committee(s)); cl-sala read 2026-10-11). Statuses are the record's own; excused absences are counted apart; nothing is inferred where a record lists no absences. 73 member(s) in the attendance record matched no listed member and are not shown.
- Generated by tools/member_profiles.py from the store each week; do not edit by hand.

## Notes

- On our ground means what the Italy edition shows: the collector's classification (taxonomy and watchlist), the edition's noise rules and mutes, and the session judge's score where it has read the vote. Titles are the source's own, verbatim.
- Party: The group the vote record names at each vote; the Senate's group spells where dati.senato.it gives them.
- In the vote table, the party's basis: 'at the vote' (the record names it), 'as listed, not at the vote' (X6: the record names none), 'group's vote, X5' (the position is DERIVED from the group's vote, never recorded per member), or the party-history source that covers the day.
- No verdicts: which way a vote cut for our side is a signed judgement (5CA) that this country does not have yet.
- 'Their vote meant' (watched and tier-1 votes) says what the member's Yes or No did, in English, by rule from the vote's wording and the bill, by the free session judge, or as a person wrote it in config/vote-meanings-it.yaml (which wins); it is what the vote did, never whose side it was on.
- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; it-camera read 12 October 2026: every Camera dei deputati mandate of the Republic (legislatures 1 to 19); the Senate is not read (its SPARQL refuses us)). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- Committees, groups and declared interests: the parliament's own open data (src/member_intel.py; it-camera-organi read 2026-10-12 (1162 row(s))). A membership is shown as recorded; a group is flagged on our ground by its name only (config/member-intel-groups.yaml). 29 row(s) named no listed member and are not shown.
- Attendance: plenary vote participation derived from the stored vote records, which list the members who did not vote (the Camera's and the Senate's records list 'assente', 'in missione' and 'presente non votante'); the 365 days to 2026-10-08. Statuses are the record's own; excused absences are counted apart; nothing is inferred where a record lists no absences.
- Generated by tools/member_profiles.py from the store each week; do not edit by hand.

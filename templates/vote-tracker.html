About this record

Free votes. Every division tracked here was a free vote: MPs voted according to personal conviction, with no party whip applied. A vote therefore reflects the member's own judgement.

“No vote recorded” is not an abstention. The Commons does not record abstentions. An MP may have no recorded vote because of illness, ministerial or constituency duties, an agreed pairing arrangement, or a deliberate decision not to vote — the record does not say which.

Who doesn't vote. By convention the Speaker and the three Deputy Speakers do not vote. Sinn Féin members do not take their seats at Westminster and so cast no votes.

New MPs. Members elected at by-elections after a division are shown as “Not yet an MP” for that division. This tool shows the record of each constituency's current MP.

Totals and lists. Results show the totals announced in the Chamber, which are the official result. The name-by-name lists are occasionally corrected afterwards, so a list may differ from the announced total by one or two — a feature of the official record, not an error in this tool.

Plain-English summaries. The “an Aye vote” lines were drafted from Hansard and the Hansard Society's Report Stage briefings. Each row links to the official division record so every entry can be checked at source. __SIGNOFF__

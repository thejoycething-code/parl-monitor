The weekly member-activity section, three ways (this week's real captures; greyed rows illustrate other activity kinds)

Common to all three: the section is renamed so it stops sounding like a PQ digest, a standing subtitle declares the full coverage, activity-type chips do the per-line signalling, and there is no watched/unwatched distinction — any parliamentarian active on our issues earns their line.

Closest to today's list shape. Strong when the week's story is who; a busy member gets one readable line, not four. The Pearson line shows the merge working.

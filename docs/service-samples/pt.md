## The index's notes on service

- Length of service: the parliament's own record of each member's periods (src/member_service.py; pt-informacaobase read 11 October 2026: every legislature from the I to the XVII, each deputy's situations and groups (InformacaoBase)). 'Serving since' is the start of the current unbroken run of periods in the chamber, as the source dates them (a dissolution, a ministerial post or a resignation breaks it); 'at least' where it starts on the first day the source covers. A source that names terms only gives no 'since'. Nothing here says whether a term is a member's first.
- DERIVED positions follow the record of who sat: where the parliament dates a member's seats and group, a member is given a group's vote only for a day they sat, and by the group they were in that day ('group's vote on the day'); 344 position(s) the store's derivation by today's group would have given were left out on that ground. A member without dated rows is derived by their latest group, as labelled.

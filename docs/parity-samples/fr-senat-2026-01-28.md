> **How to read this edition.** Items come from the France store, classified by taxonomy-fr v0.1 (approved without a native read, X4) and config/watchlist-fr.yaml. The AI judge is off (X16): nothing has been read for relevance by a model, items are ordered by tier (watched and tier 1 first), and a tier-2 match can still be noise. Titles are the source's own French, verbatim; the English around them is ours. Tallies, results and splits are the record; whether a vote helped or hurt is a human call and is never made here. Every scrutin public names every deputy's or senator's position with the group at the vote; nothing is derived, and a mise au point is counted, never applied. Migration is matched and stored but not shown. Procedural votes, the patterns in config/edition-noise-fr.yaml and Chris's mutes are left out (counted under Coverage); a watched item never is.

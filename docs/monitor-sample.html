CitizenGO Parliamentary Monitor

Internal sample pack. Live data, 4 August 2026. The MP intelligence shown here never appears in the partner edition.

An automated pipeline that watches Westminster every week (bills, questions, motions, divisions, consultations, secondary legislation), files everything a parliamentarian does on our campaign areas into a continuous intelligence ledger, and generates campaign tools from it on demand. It runs unattended on a weekly cycle; a Claude scoring pass reads each piece of evidence and places it on CitizenGO's side of the argument or against it.

1 — The 5CA generator

One command produces a pre-filled Five Column Analysis for any campaign area: all 649 sitting MPs, each placed on the ++/+/0/−/−− gradient by their strongest evidence (votes outrank speeches, speeches outrank motions, motions outrank questions; free votes outrank whipped ones), with dated, deep-linked evidence as the Comments rationale. Target stays blank — that call belongs to the campaigner. Editorial rules outrank the AI: voting against the Illegal Migration, Rwanda or Nationality and Borders enforcement measures scores badly whatever the party context, and Border Security Bill votes carry no enforcement weight. 3 — How it fits together

  • Sunday, unattended: the pipeline sweeps every source, files matched items, and a Claude pass triages them.
  • Monday, unattended: the weekly edition publishes to Slack and Asana, and a redacted partner edition (no owners, no MP intelligence) deploys to the passphrase-gated partner site.
  • Continuously: every capture also lands in the ledger with its issue areas, feeding the timelines and 5CA sheets above. Six years of history (2020–2026) is backfilled: questions, motions and signatures, 245 tagged divisions with full member breakdowns, and Hansard speeches.
  • Cost: runs on free parliamentary APIs and GitHub Actions; the AI scoring costs roughly a coffee per year.

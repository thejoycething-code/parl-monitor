## How the points work

Kind of vote: whole text +30, motion to reject or inverted +25, amendment or article -10, procedure -30; the last such vote on its bill +10. Watched bill +15 (else tier 1 +5). Values already proposed +15. Margin within 5% +20, 10% +14, 20% +8; near-unanimous (smaller side under 5%) -15. Split groups +3 each (at most +15) and +1 per two members against their group (at most +10). Meaning line ready +10 (quoting the source +3). A campaign outcome +25, on a campaign's ground +10, feeding a brief +10. Within 90 days +15, a year +10, two years +5. Left for a person by Chris +40. No longer on our ground in the store -100. Collated-only readings (an area excluded from the 5CA) are not listed: they place nobody. Ties: newer first.

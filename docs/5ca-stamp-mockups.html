The pre-2024 evidence stamp, three ways (same three MPs in every option; evidence lines are the real capture format)

The situation the stamp exists for: roughly half the Commons arrived in 2024, and for returning MPs the historic backfill mixes evidence from two parliaments. The dates already lead every evidence line; the question is whether anything louder is needed. Three specimen rows throughout: Marsham (mixed old + current evidence), Bexley (entirely pre-2024 evidence — the risk case), Calder (current parliament only, elected 2024).

Option A — No stamp: the dates carry it

Cleanest sheet. Relies on the campaigner reading "2021-07-19" and drawing their own conclusion. Bexley's ++ looks exactly as current as Calder's.

How did your member of Congress vote?

Recorded votes in the House and the Senate on the issues we track, from the Clerk's and the Senate's own roll calls, with each member's bills and floor speeches on the same ground. Search by name or by state, or filter by chamber, state and party.

Built from the Clerk of the House's and the Senate's roll-call records, Congress.gov's bill status files (sponsors and cosponsors) and GovInfo's Congressional Record. Whether a vote was for or against our side is a human judgement, made per roll call and shown only once it is signed off; a roll call without a signed reading shows the record with no judgement. Party is the party at the vote.

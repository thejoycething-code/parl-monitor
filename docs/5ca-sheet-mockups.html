Other improvements worth making

  • "Last moved" column. When a member's placement last changed. We store per-area counts each week but not per-member placements, so this needs a per-member snapshot — small table, and it turns the sheet into a genuine tracker rather than a photograph.
  • Free-vote weighting shown, not just used. The generator already prefers free votes; the sheet should say so on the row, since "voted against on a free vote" and "voted against under a whip" are different claims about a person.
  • Evidence recency. A placement resting on a 2020 speech deserves less confidence than one from June. Showing the latest date invites that judgement without us having to score it.
  • Confidence, stated plainly. One recorded vote is strong; four neutral questions are weak. A three-level marker (strong / moderate / thin) drawn from the evidence hierarchy would stop thin placements reading as firm ones.
  • Peers. The sheets cover 649 MPs and no peers, while 902 peers have evidence in the ledger and the Terminally Ill Adults Bill actually died in the Lords.
  • Print stylesheet. These get taken into meetings. One page per area, targets first, evidence trimmed.
  • Deep links both ways. The sheet links to the vote tracker; the tracker should link back to the member's placement on each issue.

Live now — four better shapes

The brief: assisted dying gets the band's whole weight, and the other backbench Bills are displayed another way — present and linked, but never competing. All four keep the evergreen petition button (no deadline in the label), the view-time day counts, and the hyperlinks to official bill pages. Real board data throughout.

Hero and a footline

The band belongs to one Bill. Big title, one-sentence story, the date block and a larger petition button on the right. Everything else in Parliament collapses to a single quiet footline under a hairline — six Bills, one unbolded sentence, all linked. Maximum contrast, minimum furniture.

Hero and a docket column

Two columns inside the band: assisted dying owns the left two-thirds; the right is a compact docket — date + linked title per line, all six other Bills, no folding needed. Keeps every Bill visible at a glance while the hierarchy stays unmistakable. Stacks on mobile.

Hero band, everything else steps outside

The strongest separation: the blue band holds only assisted dying — nothing else ever enters it. The other Bills leave the band entirely for one quiet grey line between the band and the record divider: clearly live, clearly secondary, visually ordinary. The band stops being a list and becomes a statement.

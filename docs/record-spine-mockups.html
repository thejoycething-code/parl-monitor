Keeping the card: four ways to collapse what sits under it

The big card stays exactly as it is (tile, title, status prose). These vary only the spine below — the two readings and “What happened in between”. Heights are for the closed state; every option opens to the existing full spine.

Option E — everything in one line, figures kept visible (~40px)

The readings and the in-between merge into a single disclosure line whose summary already carries the verdicts and margins — no click needed for the headline facts, one click for everything else.

# Sources cited in the debate

## The Government's equality impact assessment (Ashley Dalton, T2)

Dalton: "People from ethnic minority backgrounds, disabled people and old people are more
at risk of being coerced into an assisted death, feeling a burden, or feeling pressured to
end their life. These are the findings of the Government's own equality impact assessment."

The document: *Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill 2026: equality impact assessment*,
Department of Health and Social Care and Ministry of Justice, published 28 August 2026.

* gov.uk (HTML, 10,050 words): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill-2026-equality-impact-assessment/terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill-2026-equality-impact-assessment-html-version
* gov.uk summary: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill-2026-equality-impact-assessment/terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill-2026-equality-impact-assessment-summary
* Parliament's copy (PDF, listed on the Bills API for Bill 4157 as "Equality impact assessment - 28 August 2026"): https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/59-02/0014/2026-08-27_CLEAN_EQIA.pdf
* Its predecessor on the 2024 Bill (June 2025), for comparison: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/terminally-ill-adults-end-of-life-bill-2024-equality-impact-assessment

What the assessment says, verbatim (section in brackets):

> Evidence suggests that disabled people may be more susceptible to feeling as though they
> are a burden on those around them. [Disability — Coercion and pressure]

> Pressure is not necessarily felt or applied by other people - disabled people may feel
> subtle pressure due to attitudinal barriers or a lack of alternative appropriate services
> and support (for example, when accessing palliative care). [Disability]

> Recent discussion has also considered whether people from ethnic minority backgrounds may
> be more susceptible to coercion. This could happen as a result of structural pressures
> such as high rates of poverty, poorer healthcare access, lower quality care and
> disproportionate levels of domestic abuse committed on Black and Asian women. Structural
> pressures could subsequently result in disproportionate numbers of ethnic minority people
> choosing to have an assisted death to avoid financial hardship or escape abuse. [Disability]

> United Nations human rights experts highlight that older persons 'may feel subtly
> pressured to end their lives prematurely'. [Age]

Fair reading: Dalton's summary is supported. The assessment frames each as a risk the
Bill's safeguards "aim to mitigate", not as a finding that the Bill will cause it; the
ethnic-minority passage sits under the Disability heading, and the Race section itself
does not discuss coercion. Quote the document, not the paraphrase, in anything we publish.

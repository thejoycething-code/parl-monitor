## X thread (after the lead post)

2/ Under the law today, the woman who gives birth is the legal mother. Commissioning parents apply to the family court after six weeks, and an independent social worker checks the child's welfare. The petition called that "outdated".

3/ @ShivaniRajaMP: "A child cannot consent to a surrogacy arrangement, and they cannot understand the promises adults have made."

4/ Steve Yemm MP, Labour: the six-week period and the mother's consent are "important safeguards that I believe are worth keeping."

5/ Rebecca Smith MP asked the question the industry avoids: with "expenses" now reaching £25,000, "are we still talking about altruism or … creating a market in all but name?"

6/ Most British commissioning parents now go abroad. Applications for children born overseas have more than doubled since 2021. Tracy Gilbert MP: the women involved are those "least able to say no".

7/ The Government would not commit to legislate. Dame Diana Johnson said it is "not in a position to bring forward legislation on surrogacy reform immediately".

## Thread
1. A Commons debate this week considered a petition, signed by over 113,000 people, calling for intended parents in surrogacy arrangements to become legal parents from birth.
2. Eight MPs spoke against weakening the current safeguards — the six-week consent period, judicial oversight, and CAFCASS checks on intended parents.
3. Jonathan Hinder MP: "Pregnant women are not factories, and babies are not goods to be ordered."
4. He cited a surrogate mother's testimony: "They had the baby. They were happy. They didn't need me anymore so they told me to go home."
5. Steve Yemm MP said the mother's consent "cannot simply become irrevocable after childbirth."
6. Shivani Raja MP cited a 2023 Court of Appeal case where a surrogate won contact after a parental order was set aside.
7. Rebecca Smith MP noted the UK is now "an outlier" in Europe for permitting surrogacy at all.
8. The Minister, Dame Diana Johnson, confirmed the Government is reviewing one surrogacy agency's status on gov.uk but made no commitment to remove existing legal safeguards.
